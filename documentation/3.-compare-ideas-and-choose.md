## Weighted decision matrix

**Practice ratings.** Assume all requirements except packed height pass.

Check each requirement first: **Pass** = verified; **Check** = missing measurement; **Fail** = design change needed.

A and E pass the height limit. B, C, and D fail, regardless of their scores.

**Weight = importance:** 1 = low, 5 = high. Choose weights first; cart weight means pounds.

**Score:** 1 = weak, 3 = workable, 5 = strong; 2 and 4 fall between. **Row points = weight × score.** Add the row points.

Packed size scores trailer space; folding/setup scores the steps needed. A packs smaller. B costs less. C's simple fixed frame is easier to build and repair.

**One row:** E's build score **4 × weight 5 = 20 points**. Maximum total: **5 × 39 = 195**.

## Choose and explain

**Worked answer:** E scores **140**, ahead of A's **115**. Both pass the example height limit. Choose E for further design; check its packed height and folding joints.

**What if?** If E's build score drops from 4 to 2, it loses **10 points**. Its **130** still beats A's **115**.
